## What I've built and led

### Card programme chain
<sub>Architect · Wirex</sub>

### Blockchain RPC proxy
<sub>Head of Backend · Gateway.fm · Opera, 1inch, Gnosis, Ethereum Foundation</sub>

I designed the RPC proxy, a cache layer and rate limiting in front of the chain nodes. At peak it served 140k+ requests per second in production, four clients' traffic through one proxy at 50 ms average on the heavy requests. It became a paid product that won its clients and ran on fewer nodes, with no outages at peak.
